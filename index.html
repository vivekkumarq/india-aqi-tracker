        What the numbers mean

        All cities

        AQI uses the CPCB National Air Quality Index method (24-hour averages for PM2.5, PM10, NO₂ and SO₂; 8-hour averages for CO and O₃) applied to modelled concentrations, so values can differ from individual monitoring stations. Health advice is general guidance, not medical advice.

        Air quality and weather: Open-Meteo (Copernicus CAMS). Cities: GeoNames. City photos and summaries: Wikipedia. Basemap: Esri, HERE, Garmin, © OpenStreetMap contributors.
